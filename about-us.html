Our approach

How DevDocs is different

We blend engineering insight, user research, and strategic architecture to create documentation that helps users navigate complex products confidently.

We become power users of your product.

We learn your product by mapping real workflows, edge cases, and undocumented realities users encounter. This hands-on familiarity allows us to write with accuracy and genuine user context.

We study insights from your users and teams.

We interview SMEs, support engineers, and customer-facing teams while reviewing feedback and pain points. The patterns we uncover guide clearer structure and intuitive journeys.

We build documentation systems, not just pages.

We design scalable information architecture and documentation frameworks that support ownership, updates, and consistency for your team to maintain.

Our process

We follow a structured approach to deliver documentation that meets your exact needs.

  1. Discovery. We review your existing documentation, interview stakeholders, and understand your goals.
  2. Planning. We propose a clear structure, content plan, and timeline for delivery.
  3. Creation. We write, review, and iterate with your team until the content is polished.
  4. Delivery. We hand off production-ready documentation with ongoing support as needed.
